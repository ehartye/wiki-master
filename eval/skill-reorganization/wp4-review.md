Its PASS checks labeled execution coverage, not semantic entailment, authenticity or absence of forbidden behavior; the independent inspection and matrix below supply those judgments. WP2's independent grades remain separate and historical results are unchanged. Routing probes are selection evidence, not eleven executed workflows.

## Limits and delivery conditions

Final search/project/shared policy behavior exercised in WP2 is unchanged by later specialist work; relevant source snapshots were compared. The researcher correction has fresh affected runs. The PDF prerequisite paragraph has fresh independent source review and actual integration tests. The six-role catalog remains source-only, unreleased.

Some successful actors did not load every conditional advanced reference. Repair guessed a nonexistent validation-help filename before using the real validator; synthesis initially attempted validation before exporting its ledger, then exported and passed before close. Corrected flow recovered a stray hanging shell command without losing owned state. These are bounded observed friction, not evidence of flawless procedural compliance. Native fallback proves honest unavailable-action handling, not full app diagnostics. Fixture discovery proves independent workers and selector ordering against controlled HTTP sources, not public-web discovery quality.

No aggregate efficacy, startup-token reduction, reliability rate or live authenticated/converter coverage is inferred. The existing baselines and candidate transcripts support only these specific tasks. WP5 still requires its own fresh prepared-document review before scoped wiki publication, with foreign catalog edits preserved. Product release, merge, installation and marketplace publication remain separate actions.
